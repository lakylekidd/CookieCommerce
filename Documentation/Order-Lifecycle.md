This document describes the lifecycle of an order. 

The customer is exploring the store. They add items to their basket and perform an order. The quota determines how many cookies we are able to produce per day. Once the quota is reached, when the customer views the store, they see a message that informs them that their order will be shipped in the next day where available quota still exist. The cut off time for the quota day is 19:00, which means if a customer places an order after that time, they will be added to the next available quota date (unless we still have available quota for that specific day and their order does not exceed that).

In the evening after 19:00, I will be checking the inventory to see how many cookies we need to bake and of which type. For each type, I will create a new batch and place it on scheduled status, and once i start mixing it, i will place it on mixing status. Each batch has predetermined planned to 12 cookies (65g each). Once the batch is mixed, it is placed in the fridge so that tomorrow morning we start baking it. This is when the status switches to resting. 

In the morning I start mixing baking the batch, this is where we create a batch run (because the oven cannot bake more than 6 at a time, and maybe we do not need to bake more than 6 cookies that day). The batch run at this point shows baking. Once it's done baking, and placed outside to cool off, the run status changes to cooling. Once cooling is complete, the status is set to completed. Default batch run mins is 14, temp is 175. Once on cooling, the cookies are added under actual on the parent batch. Should i type a number under defects on the parent batch, cookies available are $ACTUAL-$DEFECT. When one of the batch runs of the batch is at Cooling status, the parent status becomes resting. That's the point when the cookies $ACTUAL-$DEFECT are available under the cookie type in the inventory.

At this stage the orders that i've set as "processing" check if they have the cookies they require available. If they don't, we should see a message on the order line indicating which types of cookies and how many are missing. These orders get assigned the cookies in order, the first order received gets assigned the cookies first, then the second order. So if I have three orders, each has chocolate chip cookies x 3 and I only have available 6 chocolate chip cookies, then the first two orders will indicate that they are ready to ship, the last order will show "3 missing chocolate chip cookies" and the ready to ship button will be grayed out.
